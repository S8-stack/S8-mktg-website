Download S8 1.2.0 S8-1.2.0.zip · recommended

S8-1.2.0.zip is the current public release: one Java stack archive for macOS, Linux, and Windows. Unzip it, point S8 at a JDK, and boot.

After you download

S8 reads machine configuration from /etc/s8-conf.properties:

/etc/s8-conf.properties 
JAVA_HOME = /Library/Java/JavaVirtualMachines/temurin-25.jdk/Contents/Home
dev.stack = /Users/you/qx/git
prod.stack = /Users/you/stack

Then follow Your first app or read how build.properties identifies a module.
